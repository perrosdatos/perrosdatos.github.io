Five portfolio concepts

Same content, five directions. Open each one and scroll / play.

1 · Neon Run

Cyberpunk city in the rain. Scroll and your avatar runs down a neon road past holographic milestones; the city shifts from magenta (Mexico) to cyan (Sweden).

Cyberpunk · AI · three.js

2 · Pixel Quest

A playable 8-bit side-scroller. Run from deserts and pyramids to snowy Swedish pines, hit ? blocks to unlock each chapter. Skills as RPG stats, projects as cartridges.

Pixel art · retro game · Canvas

3 · Cube Résumé

A real 3D Rubik's cube: each face is a section. Drag it, click a face, scramble and solve it. A voxel version of you jogs around it.

Rubik · playful · three.js

4 · J(t)

A math paper on graph paper. Your career is a 3D curve γ(t); a draughtsman's mannequin walks along it as you scroll. Lorenz attractor, skill radar, projects as theorems.

Maths · science · three.js

5 · Neural Lab

A clean robotics lab. A friendly robot walks through a neural network where each layer is a stage of your life; neurons light up as you go. Skills as a model card, projects as experiment runs.

AI · robotics · three.js
